# Zink GLES 3.1 capability checkpoint — 2026-08-13

The vendor Vulkan bridge originally exposed only OpenGL ES 2.0 through Zink.
Mesa requires instanced arrays for ES 3.0, but this Zink revision recognized
only `VK_EXT_vertex_attribute_divisor`. The Android Mali driver advertises the
newer `VK_KHR_vertex_attribute_divisor`, so the Gallium capability remained
false despite the driver supporting it.

The Zink patch normalizes the EXT and KHR spellings. It queries the expanded
KHR properties structure, reuses the ABI-compatible feature and pipeline
structures, and exposes one Gallium vertex-divisor capability. EXT remains
preferred when a driver advertises both forms.

Validated on the rootless vendor path, without Panfrost, `LD_PRELOAD`, or Mesa
version overrides:

- EGL created an OpenGL ES 3.1 context on `zink Vulkan 1.4 (Mali-G78)`.
- `egl-gles3-instancing` executed `glDrawArraysInstanced` with `GL_NO_ERROR`
  and correct pixel readback (`25,204,64,255`).
- SuperTuxKart accepted its first GLES 3.0 request, reported GLES 3.1, compiled
  its normal shaders, and rendered its menu through the vendor GPU.

The remaining Zink warnings are genuine vendor feature limits, not discovery
failures. The direct Vulkan probe reports:

```text
fillModeNonSolid=0 shaderClipDistance=0
```

These may affect applications which require wireframe polygon mode or Vulkan
clip distances, but do not invalidate the GLES 3.1 and instancing result.
