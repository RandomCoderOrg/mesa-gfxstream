Interpretation:

  YouTube selected AVC as intended, and Firefox repeatedly entered the Tensor
  VA-API path. The first surface could not be synchronized/exported because
  the Exynos MediaCodec decoder retained queued display-order output, so
  Firefox flushed that decoder and continued with software H.264. The visible
  playback in the accompanying screenshot is therefore not evidence of
  sustained hardware video decoding.

  Opening and toggling YouTube's Stats for nerds context action caused a
  significant transient stutter, hang, and visual glitch. Playback recovered
  after approximately three seconds. The 909/4078 dropped-frame counter
  includes the observed run up to the stable screenshot checkpoint; it should
  not be treated as a steady-state benchmark.
